## Positioning that follows

1. **Spline-first and editable** like SmartRoto/Mocha (artists' requirement), but **free, open source, local, and in the Non-commercial edition** too.
2. **Provenance and protection** (AI never overwrites artist work, REQ-042) and a full **project database** with undo, backups, packages — none of the surveyed tools advertise this.
3. **Gap to close:** learned matting (hair, motion blur) where Silhouette/Mocha ship Matte Refine ML (OQ-22); click-in-viewer prompting (NDK, commercial editions); measured benchmarks (OQ-13).
